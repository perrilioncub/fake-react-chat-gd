# Fake React Chat

An AI-powered fake stream chat for Geometry Dash. A whole chat full of different personalities (glazers, haters, backseaters, GO spammers...) reacts live to your attempts, deaths, new bests and completions, and argues with each other.

**Needs a Claude API key** (console.anthropic.com). Paste it in the mod settings. It's only ever sent to api.anthropic.com.
